# bild-ai-feature-practice

An invoicing app for creating, sending, and tracking invoices.

## What the app does

The app lets you create invoices, send them to clients, and see at a glance which ones are paid, pending, or overdue.

## Features

You can build invoices from reusable client details and line items, and the totals are calculated for you. Each invoice can be sent to the client straight from the app. A dashboard shows every invoice's status, so you can see what is paid, pending, or overdue. When a payment arrives, you can mark the invoice as paid in one step.

## How it works

1. Create an invoice with the client's details, line items, and a due date.
2. Send the invoice to your client.
3. Mark the invoice as paid once the payment arrives.

## Who it's for

This app is for freelancers and small businesses who need to bill clients without the overhead of full accounting software. It suits people who want to get invoices out quickly and keep track of which ones have been paid.
