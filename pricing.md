# Pricing

All prices are in convertible marks (KM) and are billed monthly. You can change or cancel your plan at any time.

| Plan | Monthly price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 15 KM | Freelancers sending a few invoices a month |
| Pro | 35 KM | Freelancers and small businesses with regular clients |

## Starter: 15 KM per month

- Up to 10 invoices a month
- Up to 5 clients
- Dashboard showing paid, pending, and overdue invoices

## Pro: 35 KM per month

- Unlimited invoices
- Unlimited clients
- Everything in Starter
