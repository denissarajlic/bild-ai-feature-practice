# Terms of Service

These terms explain who can use the invoicing app, what you are responsible for, and how to stop using it.

## Who can use the app

You can use the app if you are at least 18 years old and are billing clients for your own work or on behalf of a business you are allowed to represent. You must give accurate details when you create your account and keep your login details private.

## Your responsibility for invoices

You are responsible for every invoice you create and send through the app. This includes making sure the client details, line items, amounts, and due dates are correct, and that you have the right to bill the client for the work. The app helps you prepare and track invoices, but it does not check whether an invoice is accurate or lawful.

## How to stop using the app

You can stop using the app at any time. To close your account, email [your-email@example.com](mailto:your-email@example.com) and ask for your account to be deleted. When your account is closed, your client details, invoices, and payment dates are deleted, so save copies of any invoices you need to keep first.
