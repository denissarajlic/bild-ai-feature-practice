# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the invoicing app stores and why.

## Data the app stores

### Client details

- Name
- Email address
- Billing address

We store client details so you can address invoices and send them to your clients.

### Invoices

- Invoice number
- Issue date and due date
- Line items (description, quantity, and price)
- Totals
- Status (pending, paid, or overdue)

We store invoices so you can send them, track their status, and keep a record of what you have billed.

### Payments

- The date an invoice was marked as paid

We store payment dates so you can see when each invoice was settled.

## Data the app does not store

The app does not store card numbers, bank account details, or other payment credentials. It only records that an invoice has been paid.
