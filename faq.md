# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can my clients pay invoices through the app?

No. The app helps you create, send, and track invoices, but it does not process payments. Your clients pay you the way you agree with them, such as by bank transfer, and you mark the invoice as paid once the money arrives.

## Who can see my invoices and client details?

Only you can see your client details, invoices, and payment dates. Each client sees only the invoices you send to them. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.

## How do I close my account?

Email [your-email@example.com](mailto:your-email@example.com) and ask for your account to be deleted. Closing your account deletes your client details, invoices, and payment dates, so save copies of any invoices you need first. See the [terms of service](terms-of-service.md) for details.
